Third-party software and data
=============================

are excluded from first-party formatting because changing generated or upstream
code would make upgrades and source comparison harder.

* jQuery UI CSS 1.13.1 — MIT License. Copyright OpenJS Foundation and other
  contributors. Preferred source: https://github.com/jquery/jquery-ui/tree/1.13.1
* Mobile Detect 2.8.33 — MIT License. Copyright Serban Ghita, Nick Ilyin and
  contributors. Preferred source:
  https://github.com/serbanghita/Mobile-Detect/tree/2.8.33
  The bundled license is at includes/vendor/MobileDetect/LICENSE.txt.
* iriven GeoIPCountry and generated GeoIP data — GPL-3.0. Preferred source:
  https://github.com/iriven/GeoIPCountry
  The bundled license is at includes/vendor/GeoIP/LICENSE.
